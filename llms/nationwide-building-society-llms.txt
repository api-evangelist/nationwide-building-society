# Nationwide Building Society — Open Banking APIs

> Nationwide Building Society is the world's largest building society and one of the UK's biggest retail financial services providers. As one of the CMA9 it runs a public developer portal exposing UK Open Banking (OBIE) APIs: public, unauthenticated Open Data APIs (ATM/branch locators, product reference data) and FAPI-secured Read/Write APIs for Account Information (AIS), Payment Initiation (PIS), Confirmation of Funds (CBPII), and Variable Recurring Payments (VRP), secured with OAuth2/OIDC, mutual-TLS, and PSD2 strong customer authentication.

## Open Data APIs (public, unauthenticated — base https://openapi.nationwide.co.uk/open-banking/v2.2)
- [ATM Locator](https://developer.nationwide.co.uk/open-banking/open-data-apis): GET /atms — ATM locations, accessibility, services. Confirmed live.
- [Branch Locator](https://developer.nationwide.co.uk/open-banking/open-data-apis): GET /branches — branch locations, opening times, services.
- [Personal Current Accounts](https://developer.nationwide.co.uk/open-banking/open-data-apis): GET /personal-current-accounts — product reference data. Confirmed live.

## Read/Write APIs (FAPI-secured, mTLS — base https://api.nationwide.co.uk/open-banking/v3.1)
- [Account & Transaction Information (AIS)](https://developer.nationwide.co.uk/open-banking/account-information-apis): scope `accounts`.
- [Payment Initiation (PIS)](https://developer.nationwide.co.uk/open-banking/payment-initiation-apis): scope `payments`.
- [Confirmation of Funds (CBPII)](https://developer.nationwide.co.uk/open-banking/utility-apis): scope `fundsconfirmations`.
- [Variable Recurring Payments (VRP)](https://developer.nationwide.co.uk/open-banking/payment-initiation-apis): scope `payments`, uplifting to v4.0.
